# Music for the seed demo video

`music.opus` is the music the seed demo video's encoder lays under the video.

- Author: Monplaisir
- Album: Mothlight OST (2017)

  commercial use with no attribution; this file names the source anyway.

The file here is 90 seconds of the track starting at 0:32, where it is already steady, re-encoded
to Opus at 96 kbit/s, stereo, with all metadata removed and no other change. The video's encoder trims
it to the video's length, sets its level, and fades it in and out.
