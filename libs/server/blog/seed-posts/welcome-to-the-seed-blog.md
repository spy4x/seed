Seed is the groundwork of a SaaS, already built: sign-in with a second factor, groups with roles,
billing, realtime, an offline app and an admin area, on Deno, Postgres and Docker Compose. It is
free, MIT-licensed and built in public.

This blog is where Seed explains itself: how to start a product on it, the details that are easy
to miss, and a monthly "What's new in Seed" post on what changed. Subscribe below to get each new
post by email.
